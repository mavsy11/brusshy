# Prompts that work

Specific beats vague. Name the part, the change and the reason. These work in your AI's chat, in Brusshy's canvas chat, or as notes pinned on the logo.

## Starting

- *"Design a logo for **Northwind**, a specialty coffee roaster in Lisbon. Customers are young professionals. Modern and warm, not rustic. Must work as an app icon and a 16 px favicon. One idea, symbol + wordmark."*
- *"Give me 4 directions for **Playmark**, a screen recorder: a camera, a play button, a recording dot, and one wildcard. Keep each one simple enough for an app icon."*
- *"Turn my sketch into a clean vector logo. Keep the proportions; fix the symmetry."*
- *"Vectorize the photo of our ceramic mug as a solid silhouette, then redraw it as a minimal icon."*

## Refining

- *"Make the bowl of the P 10% larger and the stem thinner, keep the lens as is."*
- *"The gap between the dot and the play closes at 16 px — open it up."*
- *"Center the play optically in the body."*
- *"Join these three bars into one shape with square inner corners and rounded outer corners."*
- *"Tighten the wordmark tracking and scale the mark to 1.8× the cap height."*

## Building the system

- *"Make a horizontal lockup and a version for dark backgrounds."*
- *"Generate a palette from the logo with a cool blue primary, then show me the colorways."*
- *"Suggest a body font that pairs with Permanent Marker and save the type system."*
- *"Give the logo a subtle entrance animation (under 1 s) and export an animated SVG."*
- *"Show it on a business card and as an app icon."*
- *"Critique the active version and pin the fixes as notes."*

## Tips

- Pin a **note** on the exact part instead of describing where it is.
- **Lock** the parts you love (lock-with-spark toggle) so the AI never touches them.
- Write a **Brief** once; the AI reads it before every design.
- Ask for **variants** when you're exploring, **duplicates** when you're refining.
